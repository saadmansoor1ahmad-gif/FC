Finance Transformation

Rebuilding reporting and controls around a new ERP to turn month-end from a two-week scramble into a same-week close.

Challenge

Slow reporting and limited visibility

Following a financial merger and integration into a larger multinational group, the finance function inherited two sets of systems that had never been properly connected. Data lived in disconnected ERP and legacy platforms, reconciliations were largely manual, and intercompany mismatches between entities were a recurring drag on every close.

The practical effect was a month-end that routinely ran to Day 9 — by the time consolidated numbers reached the Board, they were describing a month that had already moved on. Decisions on pricing, cost and investment were being made on data that was structurally out of date.

Approach

ERP integration and KPI reporting

Started with the team and the process rather than the software: restructured the finance function and streamlined the close itself, removing duplicated steps and clarifying who owned each reconciliation. In parallel, integrated the ERP, MS Dynamics and Linux-based systems via APIs, so data moved between platforms automatically instead of through manual exports and re-keying.

A faster close isn't a systems project — it's a systems project with a reporting discipline built around it.

Led finance workstreams across the wider ERP, supply chain and payroll & HR transformation programmes to keep the finance data model aligned with changes elsewhere in the business, and built a monthly management accounts and forecasting pack tied to daily operational KPIs — so the close produced a decision-ready pack, not just a set of numbers.

  • Finance team restructured and month-end process streamlined
  • ERP, MS Dynamics and Linux systems integrated via APIs
  • Consolidation and intercompany controls reviewed and retrained across the team
  • Finance workstreams led across ERP, supply chain and payroll transformation
  • Monthly management accounts and forecasting pack rebuilt around daily KPIs
Result

Month-end close reduced from Day 9 to Day 1

Day 9 → Day 1

Monthly close cut from Day 9 to Day 1, with consolidated financial statements presented by Day 3 — giving the Board a current, reliable view of performance in time to actually act on it, rather than a retrospective account of a month already closed. Clean audits were achieved throughout, with the tighter controls holding up under external scrutiny.
