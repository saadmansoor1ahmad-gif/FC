Case Study · Systems & Reporting

Finance Transformation

Rebuilding reporting and controls around a new ERP to turn month-end from a two-week scramble into a same-week close.

Challenge

Slow reporting and limited visibility

Following a merger, the finance function inherited disconnected systems and manual processes. Month-end close routinely ran to Day 9, consolidated numbers arrived too late to inform decisions, and intercompany mismatches were a recurring drag on the close.

Approach

ERP integration and KPI reporting

Restructured the finance team and streamlined the close process end to end. Integrated ERP, MS Dynamics and Linux-based systems via APIs to improve data integrity, led finance workstreams across ERP, supply chain and payroll transformation, and built a monthly management accounts and forecasting pack tied to daily operational KPIs.

Result

Month-end close reduced from Day 9 to Day 1

Day 9 → Day 1

Monthly close cut from Day 9 to Day 1, with consolidated financial statements presented by Day 3 — giving the Board a current, reliable view of performance in time to act on it.
