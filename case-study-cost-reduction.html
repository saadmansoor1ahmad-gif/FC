Case Study · Cost & Controls

£1.2M Annual Savings

Bringing a rising cost base under control through restructuring and sharper management reporting.

Challenge

Rising operating cost base

Operating costs had grown ahead of revenue, with limited visibility into which cost lines were driving the increase. Management lacked the reporting detail needed to make targeted decisions, and cost ownership sat unclearly across departments.

Approach

Cost restructuring and management reporting

Restructured the operating cost base, reworking pricing and costing methodology and reviewing supply chain value-for-money processes with tighter controls. Rebuilt management reporting so cost drivers were visible by department and service line, and delivered cost-management training so operational managers owned their numbers rather than finance chasing them after the fact.

Result

£1.2M recurring savings

£1,200,000 / year

Recurring annual savings delivered and embedded into the run-rate cost base, with reporting in place to hold the gains rather than let costs drift back up.
