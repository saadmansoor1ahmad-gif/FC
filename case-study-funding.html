Case Study · Capital & Treasury

£26M Funding Programme

Securing strategic capital through consortium-based financing and disciplined treasury management.

Challenge

Capital required for strategic investment

A manufacturing business within a multinational group needed to fund a major asset investment programme — capital equipment and infrastructure to support growth — without straining working capital or overleveraging the balance sheet.

Approach

Structured consortium funding and treasury management

Led the CAPEX budgeting process alongside treasury and funding management, structuring a consortium-based financing arrangement to spread risk across funding partners. Built a rolling cashflow forecast to give lenders confidence in repayment capacity, and managed the Group's Forex exposure alongside the funding process.

Result

£26M secured

£26,000,000

Assets worth £26m+ secured for the business, funding the investment programme while keeping treasury and covenant positions healthy throughout.
