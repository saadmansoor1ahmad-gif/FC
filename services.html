Services

Senior finance leadership, structured around what your business actually needs — from a few days a month to a full transformation programme.

01 · Advisory

Fractional CFO

Board-level financial strategy without the cost of a full-time hire. I work alongside MDs and Boards on strategic decision-making, investment appraisal, funding strategy and performance management — typically on a part-time or retained basis, scaled to the size of the business.

02 · Leadership

Finance Director

Hands-on leadership of the finance function: building the team, rewriting reporting cycles, tightening controls, and putting in place the governance a growing or newly-acquired business needs. Includes statutory reporting, IFRS conversion, audit management and Board reporting.

03 · Performance

FP&A

Budgeting, rolling forecasts, and KPI frameworks that connect operational performance to financial outcomes. Margin analysis, revenue channel reporting and board-ready packs that give management teams a clear, current view of the business.

04 · Transactions

Mergers & Acquisitions

Financial due diligence, deal structuring and post-merger integration. Experience leading multiple acquisitions across EMEA, including full financial merger and integration into a multinational group.

05 · Treasury

Cash Flow & Treasury

Working capital optimisation, FX exposure management, and rolling cashflow forecasting (up to 15 weeks). Capital investment planning and funding structuring — including consortium-based financing for major asset investment.

06 · Systems

ERP Transformation

Leading finance workstreams through ERP, supply chain and payroll system change. Practical experience integrating SAP, MS Dynamics, Business Central, Sage and Linux-based systems via APIs to improve data integrity and cut reporting time.
